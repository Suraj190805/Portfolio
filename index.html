IoT vehicular safety system

SELECTED WORK IOT & EMBEDDED SYSTEMS

Alerto — IoT Accident Detection

IoT device detecting vehicular collisions via accelerometer data and autonomously dispatching real-time GPS coordinates to emergency contacts via SMS/call — potentially saving lives in critical moments.

CHALLENGE

Road accidents in remote areas often go unreported for critical minutes. Victims may be unconscious and unable to call for help, making automated detection essential.

IMPACT

Created a low-cost, reliable safety device with manual override for false alerts, demonstrating how embedded systems can directly address life-threatening real-world problems.
